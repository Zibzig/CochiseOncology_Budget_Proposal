How we'll run it

Start small
Search ads only
Ads appear only when someone types a relevant search. No banners, no social media.
One campaign per condition
Osteoarthritis, breast, prostate, hematology each measured on their own, so we can stop what doesn't work.
Phone first
Most oncology inquiries are calls. Call tracking is live before the first dollar is spent, and the front desk asks "how did you hear about us?"
90-day check
Board sees cost per inquiry and cost per new patient by service line. Continue, adjust, or stop.

Plain-English terms

Click
One person tapping our ad after searching. We pay per click, not per view.
Cost per click
What Google charges for one click. Local specialty-medicine terms run roughly $15–30.
Inquiry
A phone call or form from a real person asking about treatment.
New patient
An inquiry that becomes a scheduled, treated patient.
Return
Estimated revenue from those new patients, using the per-patient values above.
Break-even
How many new patients a month pay for the ad spend on their own.
SEO
Making our website show up in free search results. Slow but lasting; the ads are the fast lane while SEO builds.
LDRT
Low-dose radiation therapy, our osteoarthritis treatment. Rare enough that people travel for it.

Per-patient values are rough; refine after Tuesday's data.
